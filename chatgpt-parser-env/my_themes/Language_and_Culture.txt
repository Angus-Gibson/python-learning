---

(Note: The repeated "YOU" at the end indicates it's a yes/no question through ASL grammar.)

3. **ALONE** – Use the index finger (number 1 handshape) and make a small circular motion in front of your chest, showing solitude.
4. **YOU?** – Point again at the person with a raised brow (facial expression signals a yes/no question).
